Name: Daily invite engine
Schedule: every day at 09:00 (cron: 0 9 * * *)
Tools: Supabase MCP (leads-crm project enybzqvjrjdpepikjmwl)
Never touch LinkedIn — CRM only.

Daily instructions:

1) Contact top-up
- Query companies with score >= 6 that have zero contacts.
- For each (prioritize HOT/GOOD, highest score), research founder or CTO and their LinkedIn URL (public web search).
- Insert into public.contacts: company_id, name, title, linkedin_url, is_primary=true, linkedin_verified=false.
- Target: keep at least 15 contacts with linkedin_url who are not blocked by an existing invite.

2) Build today's invite queue
- Call RPC public.invite_capacity().
- remaining = min(remaining_today, remaining_week). If 0, skip queue build.
- Find eligible contacts: have linkedin_url; company has no invite with status in (queued, pending, accepted); contact has no invite in the last 21 days.
- Prefer primary contacts, then score desc, temperature HOT→GOOD→MAYBE.
- Insert up to `remaining` rows into public.outreach:
  kind='invite', status='queued', channel='linkedin',
  subject='LinkedIn invite — {name}',
  body='Queued for today\'s Daily 10. Send without a note.'

3) Draft messages for newly accepted invites
- Find outreach where kind='invite' and status='accepted' and updated/created recently, with no kind='message' row yet for that contact_id in status draft/approved/sent.
- For each, load company (name, funding, hiring_roles, offer_type, trigger_type) and contact name.
- Insert outreach kind='message', status='draft', channel='linkedin' with a short LinkedIn DM (<400 chars):
  thanks for connecting + one trigger line + one offer line + soft ask for a short chat.

4) Report summary
- contacts added, invites queued, pending invite count, message drafts waiting for approval.
- Remind: user runs "run today's batch" in Cursor to send invites on LinkedIn.
